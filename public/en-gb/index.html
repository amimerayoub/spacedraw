Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How do you play the SpaceDraw draw and guess game?

Choose an avatar, pick a word category, and take turns sketching the secret prompt or guessing in the chat before the timer runs out.

Is SpaceDraw completely free with no sign-up?

Yes. You can play immediately in your web browser without creating an account or downloading any software.

Can I practise drawing and guessing on my own?

Yes, our Practice Mode and simulated players let you enjoy quick rounds solo whenever you like.

Does the game work on mobile browsers?

Absolutely. SpaceDraw is built for smooth touch sketching on mobile phones and tablets as well as desktop computers.

Can I customise the difficulty of the words?

Yes, you can select Easy, Medium, or Hard word packs across multiple themed categories.
